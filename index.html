Customer success playbook

Discovery: Ask about the customer's goals, environment and level of familiarity with Linux.

Enablement: Share trusted resources, confirm understanding and avoid assumptions.

Follow-up: Check whether the customer achieved their intended outcome and capture any blockers.

Escalation: Route security, infrastructure or account-specific issues to qualified technical support.
